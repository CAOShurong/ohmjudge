# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be precise, constructive, and respectful. Critique methods and evidence rather
than people. Welcome questions from researchers, students, and practitioners
with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing
private information, deliberate benchmark manipulation, and sustained bad-faith
disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in context. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban participants when needed
to protect the project community.

This compact policy is adapted from the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
